 # Software Tester

## Job Description

We are seeking a detail-oriented Software Tester to help ensure our applications are reliable, functional, and user-friendly. The successful candidate will design and execute tests, identify defects, and work with developers to resolve issues.

## Responsibilities

- Review requirements and create test plans and test cases.
- Perform manual and automated testing of software applications.
- Identify, document, and track bugs through resolution.
- Conduct regression, integration, and usability testing.
- Collaborate with developers, product managers, and other team members.
- Provide clear reports on test results and product quality.

## Requirements

- Basic understanding of software testing principles and methodologies.
- Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills.
- Good written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Familiarity with issue-tracking tools is a plus.
- Experience with test automation is desirable but not required.

## Benefits

- Competitive salary and benefits package.
- Opportunities for professional development.
- Collaborative and supportive work environment.
